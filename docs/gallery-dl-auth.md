## App Settings Mapping

- `Cookies`: use `gallery-dl Cookies File` or `gallery-dl Browser Cookies`.
- `OAuth`: use `gallery-dl Extra Options` for extractor-specific OAuth/client settings.
- `API Key`: use `gallery-dl Extra Options` with `extractor.site.key=value` lines.
- `Supported`: use the site-appropriate setting: username/password, cookies, or extra options.
- `Required`: provide the site-appropriate required credential before downloading.
